# 11 - Attendance from the provider, correctable by an instructor

**What to build:** Attendance is captured from the meeting provider's participation log rather than transcribed by hand. An instructor can correct it where the capture is wrong, and the corrected value wins and records who corrected it.

Manual marking stays available for providers that expose no participation data.

A student can see their own attendance record, which is what they will be asked for if their college checks.

**Blocked by:** 07 - Recurring schedule

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Attendance appears after a live session without an instructor marking it
- [ ] An instructor can correct an attendance record, and the correction wins over the captured value
- [ ] A correction records who made it
- [ ] Manual marking is available where the provider exposes no participation data
- [ ] A student can see their own attendance across a batch and cannot see anyone else's
