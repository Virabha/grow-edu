# 13 - Doubts anchored to a lesson or a question, with an image

**What to build:** A doubt is asked against a target - the batch, a specific lesson, or a specific question in a test - rather than only at batch level. The anchor determines the context shown to whoever answers, and who can see it.

A student can attach a photograph or screenshot to show their working instead of describing it. An answer can carry an image too.

A student can see doubts other students in their batch have asked, so they do not wait for an answer that already exists.

**Blocked by:** None - can start immediately

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A doubt can be anchored to a batch, a lesson, or a question in a test
- [ ] The answerer sees the anchored context without navigating away
- [ ] An image can be attached to a doubt and to an answer
- [ ] A student sees other students' doubts in their own batch and none from any other batch
- [ ] A student is notified when their doubt is answered, without checking repeatedly
