# 14 - Instructor doubt inbox across all batches, with reassignment

**What to build:** An instructor gets one inbox of every unanswered doubt across all the batches they are assigned to, ordered by age, so the oldest question does not rot unseen.

Each doubt shows how long it has been waiting against a configurable response target. An instructor can reassign a doubt to a colleague assigned to the same batch, so a question outside their subject reaches whoever can answer it.

**Blocked by:** 13 - Anchored doubts

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An instructor sees unanswered doubts from every batch they are assigned to, and none from batches they are not
- [ ] The inbox is ordered by how long each doubt has waited
- [ ] Elapsed time is shown against a response target that the owner can configure
- [ ] A doubt can be reassigned to another instructor on the same batch
- [ ] A doubt cannot be reassigned to someone who is not assigned to that batch
