# 15 - Promote an answer to the whole batch

**What to build:** An instructor can publish a good answer to the whole cohort, so the same question is not asked fifty times.

Promotion converts a resolved doubt into content the batch can see. The student who asked is not identified unless they consent.

**Blocked by:** 13 - Anchored doubts

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A resolved doubt can be promoted so the whole batch can see it
- [ ] A promoted answer does not identify the asker unless the asker consented
- [ ] The batch can browse promoted answers without going through the doubt inbox
- [ ] Promoting a doubt does not notify the batch as if it were a new announcement unless chosen
