# 34 - Written feedback on a student

**What to build:** The owner or an instructor can leave written feedback on a student's performance that the student can read, so giving feedback is part of the product rather than something done over the phone.

**Blocked by:** 03 - Notification infrastructure

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Staff can leave written feedback against a student on a batch
- [ ] The student can read feedback left for them and cannot read anyone else's
- [ ] The student is notified when feedback is left
- [ ] Feedback records who wrote it and when
