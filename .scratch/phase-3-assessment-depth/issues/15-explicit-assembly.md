# 15 - Explicit test assembly

**What to build:** An instructor builds a test by picking questions from the bank. Assembly becomes selection rather than authoring.

Adding a grouped question adds its whole group; a group cannot be partially served.

**Blocked by:** 08, 14

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Questions are added to a test by reference and appear in the order they were placed
- [ ] Adding one member of a group adds the whole group, ordered
- [ ] Removing one member of a group removes the group
- [ ] A retired question cannot be added
- [ ] The same question cannot be added to one test twice
