# 19 - Previous-year papers

**What to build:** Previous-year papers are ordinary tests assembled from imported questions, distinguished by metadata — exam, year, paper — rather than by a separate mechanism.

The work here is a metadata surface and a way to find them, not a new engine.

**Blocked by:** 15

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A test can be marked as a previous-year paper carrying its exam, year and paper label
- [ ] Students can list and filter available papers by exam and year
- [ ] A paper attempts and scores through exactly the same path as any other test
- [ ] A paper is not listed as a batch test unless it is also assigned to that batch
- [ ] The metadata is optional and its absence changes nothing
