# 26 - Grading a written answer against a rubric

**What to build:** An instructor records a value per rubric criterion plus a comment; the mark for that answer is derived from the criteria rather than typed.

The submission-and-grading model established in Phase 1 serves this; it is not reimplemented.

**Blocked by:** 24, 25

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A recorded grade stores each criterion value and derives the total
- [ ] Grading the last pending answer finalises the attempt's score
- [ ] The finalised score is the provisional objective score plus the graded marks
- [ ] A student sees the criterion breakdown, not only the total
- [ ] A grade records its actor and the time it was recorded
