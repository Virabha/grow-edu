# 28 - Spoken and recorded feedback

**What to build:** An instructor can attach audio or screen-captured video feedback to a graded answer. It is uploaded, transcoded and attached through the existing media pipeline rather than a parallel one.

**Blocked by:** 26

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Feedback media is attached to a specific graded answer, not to the attempt as a whole
- [ ] The student can retrieve it with their result
- [ ] Transcoding runs through the existing pipeline and the attachment appears when it completes
- [ ] A failed transcode leaves the written grade intact
- [ ] Only the student who owns the attempt and staff can retrieve it
