# 32 - Result: time per question against the cohort

**What to build:** A student sees how long they spent on each question next to the cohort average, so they learn where they are wasting time.

**Blocked by:** 02, 31

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Each question shows the student's time and the cohort mean for that question in that test
- [ ] The cohort mean excludes attempts still in progress
- [ ] A test with a single attempt reports the mean as that attempt rather than failing
- [ ] Skipped questions are excluded from the cohort mean
- [ ] A student cannot infer another individual's timing from the figures
