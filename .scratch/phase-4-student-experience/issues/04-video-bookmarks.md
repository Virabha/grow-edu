# 04 - Timestamped bookmarks with notes

**What to build:** A student can bookmark a moment in a video and attach a note, then jump straight back to it. Revision becomes targeted rather than rewatching.

**Blocked by:** 03

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A bookmark records the lesson, the timestamp and an optional note
- [ ] Bookmarks list in timestamp order for a lesson and in recency order across a batch
- [ ] A student sees only their own bookmarks
- [ ] A bookmark survives the lesson's video being re-encoded
- [ ] Deleting a bookmark removes it and nothing else
