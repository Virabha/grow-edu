# 07 - Audio renditions from the existing pipeline

**What to build:** An audio-only rendition of a lecture, produced by the EXISTING encoding pipeline rather than uploaded separately, so a student can revise while commuting.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An audio rendition is requested through the existing encoding path, not a parallel one
- [ ] The rendition is attached to the same lesson, not a duplicate lesson
- [ ] Progress on the audio rendition is the same progress as the video
- [ ] A lesson without a rendition reports its absence rather than failing
- [ ] Requesting a rendition twice does not encode twice
