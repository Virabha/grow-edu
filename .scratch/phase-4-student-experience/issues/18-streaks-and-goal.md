# 18 - Streaks and the daily goal

**What to build:** A streak counts consecutive days of MEETING A DAILY GOAL, not of opening the application. The goal is configurable, and its defaults are owner-managed configuration.

**Blocked by:** 17

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A day counts toward the streak only when the goal is met
- [ ] Opening the app without meeting the goal does not extend the streak
- [ ] A missed day breaks the streak; the next qualifying day starts a new one
- [ ] The goal is per student, editable, and defaults from owner configuration
- [ ] Streak evaluation is driven by the injected clock, including across a day boundary
