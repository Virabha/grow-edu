# 21 - Batch discussion feed

**What to build:** A moderated discussion feed scoped to a batch, with instructors visible in it so discussion is guided rather than unmoderated.

ALL community content is visible to instructors and administrators of that batch. That visibility is what makes moderation possible at all.

**Blocked by:** None - can start immediately

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A student can post and reply in the feed of a batch they are enrolled in
- [ ] A student cannot read or post to another batch's feed
- [ ] Instructor posts are distinguishable from student posts
- [ ] Instructors and admins of that batch can read everything in it
- [ ] A removed post stops appearing but is retained for moderation review
