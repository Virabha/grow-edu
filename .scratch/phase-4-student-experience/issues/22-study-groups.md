# 22 - Study groups

**What to build:** Small peer study groups, BOUNDED IN SIZE and SCOPED TO A BATCH. A group spanning batches has no owner responsible for moderating it.

There is no direct messaging anywhere in this phase. That is a safeguarding decision, not a feature preference.

**Blocked by:** 21

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A group belongs to exactly one batch and cannot span batches
- [ ] Group size is capped and the cap is owner-managed configuration
- [ ] A student cannot read a group they do not belong to
- [ ] Instructors and admins of the batch can read every group in it
- [ ] No interface anywhere permits a private message between two students
