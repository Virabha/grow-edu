# 24 - Filterable catalogue

**What to build:** A public catalogue of batches filterable by goal, subject, language and start date — the commercial front door.

**Blocked by:** None - can start immediately

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The catalogue is readable without signing in
- [ ] Filters compose: goal and language together narrow rather than widen
- [ ] Unpublished, deleted and corporate-only batches never appear
- [ ] Results are paginated and stable under a repeated request
- [ ] No enrolment, pricing internals or roster data leaks through the public surface
