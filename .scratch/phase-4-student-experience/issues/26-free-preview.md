# 26 - Free preview lessons

**What to build:** A free preview is a FLAG on a curriculum item plus an access-check exception, evaluated by the single access module from Phase 1. It is not a separate content path.

**This is a paywall boundary and the highest-risk item in the phase.**

**Blocked by:** 10, 24

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A preview lesson is reachable without enrolment
- [ ] No adjacent content becomes reachable with it — not the next lesson, not the batch's resources, not its tests
- [ ] The exception is evaluated by the existing access module, not by a second rule
- [ ] Removing the flag immediately closes access
- [ ] A preview lesson's video is still delivered by signed URL, not proxied
