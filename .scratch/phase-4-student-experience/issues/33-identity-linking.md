# 33 - Identity linking onto one account

**What to build:** Phone and Google are ADDITIONAL IDENTITY METHODS ON ONE ACCOUNT, not separate accounts. A student who signs up by phone and later by Google must not become two students.

Account merging built in Phase 1 is what makes this safe.

**Blocked by:** 31, 32

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Phone and Google sign-in for the same person resolve to one account
- [ ] A second method cannot claim an account belonging to someone else
- [ ] Linking requires proving control of both identities
- [ ] Unlinking cannot leave an account with no way to sign in
- [ ] A linked account keeps one progress history, not two
