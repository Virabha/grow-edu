# 41 - Openings, applications and status

**What to build:** Openings, applications, status tracking, and employer visibility into portfolios students have published.

**Deliberately minimal. Employer accounts, screening and placement fees are explicitly out of scope** — a different business with different obligations. Employer visibility extends only to portfolios the student has already published.

**Blocked by:** nothing

**Status:** not-started

- [ ] The owner can post an opening and see who applied
- [ ] A student can apply and track their application status
- [ ] Employer visibility extends only to published portfolios
- [ ] An unpublished portfolio is not reachable through the job board
- [ ] A student cannot see another student's applications
